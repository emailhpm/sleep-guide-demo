Your ritual

Three steps, about a minute, at the same time every night. Sleepwhale is “formulated for nightly use as part of a consistent bedtime routine”, so the ritual matters as much as the drops.

The three steps

  1. 10 minutes before bedTake 10 minutes before bed timeShake well, then fill the dropper to 1 mL. Hard to fill? Squeeze the bulb, then release it slowly in the liquid.
  2. 1 minuteDrop Sleepwhale under tongue, hold, then swallowAbout a minute, no water needed. Tastes bitter? Shake well every time.
  3. Lights outGet cozy, close eyes, and drift offSleepwhale says “you should feel the effects within 10-15 minutes.”

    Check the basics first: 10 minutes before bed, under your tongue, after a good shake. A late meal can slow it down. And give it time: whether your sleep itself is improving “can take longer, sometimes up to several weeks.”

    “Because we use a water-soluble formula instead of oil-based (aka every other product), you should feel the effects within 10-15 minutes. Depending on your diet, how close you eat before bed time, you can expect this estimated figure to change and be a bit longer.”

    What if I still wake up at 3 am?

    “Take your drops 10 minutes before bed, not in the middle of the night. Sleepwhale is built to keep working through the second half of the night, when cortisol starts rising.” If it keeps happening, the serving check shows how to fine-tune. Serving check

    Will this make me groggy?

    “No. Our carefully selected ingredients work in harmony to create a smooth and restful sleep that leaves you waking up refreshed. We’re so confident we offer a 30 night risk-free trial.” If you do feel groggy, go down by 0.25 mL and take your drops right at bedtime.

    Will I feel high?

    No. “Our hemp is broad spectrum, which means it contains no THC. No high, no hangover.” The lab found no THC either. Lab results

    Can I take this every night?

    Yes, that's how it's meant to be taken: “Sleepwhale is formulated for nightly use as part of a consistent bedtime routine.”

    It tastes bitter. What helps?

    Shake the bottle well every time. If it clumps, customers sit the bottle in hot water for a minute and shake it again. One customer turned it into a cue: “Even the bitterness is kind of like a shock to my system to remind me that it’s time to slow down and start relaxing.”

    Is Sleepwhale right for me?

    It's for “Anyone over the age of 18 who is not currently pregnant or breastfeeding.” If you're over 65, and especially if you take prescription medications, Sleepwhale recommends talking to your doctor first.

    On HRT or other medication: “Many people take Sleepwhale alongside HRT and other medication. If you're on prescriptions or have a health condition, check with your doctor or pharmacist first.”

    Can I take it when I travel?

    The bottle holds 1 oz (30 mL), so it fits in your carry-on. Take your drops 10 minutes before bed where you are, not at home time. Sleepwhale ships within the United States, so pack enough, and check the rules for hemp products at your destination before you fly.

    I compete in a WADA-tested sport. Anything to know?

    Yes. Your drops contain CBN, and WADA prohibits all natural and synthetic cannabinoids except CBD in competition. Out of competition they are allowed. Check with your federation before you compete.

    Your night has more than one chapter

    Light sleep, deep sleep, REM. Discover what happens after you drift off — and where Sleepwhale fits in.

    You close your eyes. The next thing you know, the alarm goes off.

    But between those two moments, your sleep moves through different stages, from drifting off to deep sleep and dreaming. And it repeats that journey throughout the night.

    Here’s a closer look at what happens after lights out.

    1. N1

      Drifting off

      That in-between feeling when you’re no longer fully awake? This is the first, lightest stage of sleep.

    2. N2

      Settling into sleep

      You’re now asleep, though not yet in the deepest stage. It’s another part of the cycle your body returns to during the night.

    3. N3

      Deep sleep

      Also called slow-wave sleep, this is your deepest non-REM stage. More of it usually happens earlier in the night.

    4. REM

      When dreams get vivid

      Your brain becomes more active, and vivid dreams often happen here. REM periods tend to be longer later in the night.

    The cycle repeats through the night.
